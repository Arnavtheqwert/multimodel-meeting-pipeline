# Meeting Record

## Summary
The Oregon Tourism Commission met on May 14 to discuss the recruitment of a new Executive Director. After introductions and confirming compliance with public‑meeting notice requirements, the commission voted to advance three candidates—Kavon Dugan, Stacy Shaw, and Kate Sinner—to the next interview round.
